# Privacy policy

- The app accesses only the Google Contacts of its owner, with read-only permission (scope `contacts.readonly`).
- Data is stored on the owner's private server and processed only by an AI assistant operated by the owner. It is never shared with or sold to third parties and never used for advertising.
- The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Contact: the repository owner on GitHub.

## Italiano

L'app legge solo i contatti Google del titolare, in sola lettura. I dati restano sul suo server privato e sono elaborati solo da un assistente AI gestito da lui; non vengono condivisi, ceduti né usati per pubblicità. L'accesso si revoca in ogni momento da https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
